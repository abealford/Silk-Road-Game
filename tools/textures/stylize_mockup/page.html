2. Ground blending

A 15 × 7.5 m patch of ground, seen from above, with meadow, dry steppe, sand, a rock outcrop, a dirt road and the edge of a paved square. Top: how v22.2c blends now (photo textures, straight crossfades along the terrain grid, hard road and paving edges). Bottom: stylized textures with height-based blending, broken-up borders, matched colours and a dirt fringe at the paving.

Now (v22.2c)
New blending
Height blend: each texture's bumps decide who wins at a border, so grass grows between paving stones first and sand fills the low spots.
Border width and noise: how wide the mixing zone is, and how much it wanders instead of following the terrain grid.
Colour harmony: evens out brightness and contrast between textures shot by different photographers.
Road and paving fringe: worn earth around road and paving edges instead of a hard cut.
What a flat mockup can't show: the game's sky light, fog, distance and the region and season colours that tint every texture. Once you've picked settings, one short test build confirms them in the world.
